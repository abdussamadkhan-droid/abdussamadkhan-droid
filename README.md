## 🤖 AI Career Copilot

### **An AI-powered career assistant designed around the complete career-preparation journey.**

Choosing a career path and preparing for opportunities involves much more than creating a resume.

The **AI Career Copilot** brings several career-preparation capabilities together into one application.

### 🔥 What It Does

```text
             📄 RESUME
                 │
                 ▼
          🔍 RESUME ANALYSIS
                 │
                 ▼
          🎯 JOB MATCHING
                 │
                 ▼
         🧠 SKILL GAP ANALYSIS
                 │
                 ▼
       ✍️ AI CAREER CONTENT
                 │
                 ▼
        🎤 INTERVIEW PREPARATION
                 │
                 ▼
           🗺️ CAREER GUIDANCE
```

### ✨ Key Features

📄 **Resume Analysis**
Understand and analyze information from a user's resume.

🎯 **Job Matching**
Compare candidate skills against job requirements.

🧠 **Skill Gap Analysis**
Identify matched skills, missing skills, and areas requiring improvement.

✍️ **AI-Assisted Career Content**
Generate useful career-oriented content with AI assistance.

🎤 **Interview Preparation**
Support technical and career preparation through AI-generated guidance.

🗺️ **Career Guidance**
Provide a structured direction for continuous career improvement.

### 🛠️ Technologies

`Python` `AI` `LLM` `Streamlit` `APIs` `Authentication` `Document Processing`
